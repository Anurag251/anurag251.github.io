Game Uploader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Game Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, Anurag, to upload his own gaming videos to his own TikTok account.

What data the app uses

  • The TikTok access token for the owner's account, created when the owner logs in through TikTok Login Kit.
  • Basic profile info of the owner's account (display name, open ID) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The owner's own video files and captions, which are sent to TikTok to be uploaded.

How it is stored

  • Everything is stored only on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.
  • No data from any other TikTok user is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Deleting data

  • The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions > Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file.
